Why?
A 3am “𝘞𝘩𝘢𝘵 𝘪𝘧?” to prove I could.
Because hard is worth doing, every time.
Because I’d rather do, than wonder why.

Sub-zero weather, starting at 2:30am.
The first 2 hours went by like nothing.
The last 2 were spent in agony. Just one foot in front of the other, it was all I had.

I wouldn’t walk this path twice, but I’d do it for the first time, every time.

Now I’ll spend the week recovering…
and thinking about the stories I’ll tell 20 years from now.

But this story comes with a reminder:

𝗡𝗼𝘁 𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗿𝘆 𝗱𝗲𝗰𝗶𝘀𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗻𝗲𝗲𝗱𝘀 𝘁𝗼 𝗯𝗲 𝘀𝗺𝗮𝗿𝘁.
𝗦𝗼𝗺𝗲𝘁𝗶𝗺𝗲𝘀, 𝗶𝘁 𝗷𝘂𝘀𝘁 𝗻𝗲𝗲𝗱𝘀 𝘁𝗼 𝗯𝗲 𝗱𝗼𝗻𝗲.

just one 𝘩𝘰𝘯𝘦𝘴𝘵 desire.
