You can make a case that times have changed. We obviously don't live in the jungles anymore, surrounded by tigers.

But I disagree. Sure, the consequences to "Alone" has never been lower. You could even find comfort in it!

That's the beautiful part of being human.

I changed my mind. I no longer believe that comfort in solitude should be a goal.
