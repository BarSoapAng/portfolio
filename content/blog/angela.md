## Who is Angela

At her core, <span>Angela loves creating</span>. For some time, that meant art, then games, then robots, and now code and food sometimes.

She thinks the beautiful part about building is that she gets to put a piece of herself in every creation. Every product decision, every small UI/UX consideration, every implementation detail comes from this long lineage of her personally lived experiences. And a lot of the times, there are very unique considerations she thought about when making those small decisions.

Like the color pink! Pink actually isn't her favourite color -- although this whole site is pink, her laptop and vscode is pink themed -- her favourite color is purple. And if someone asked her why the color pink, she'd have a pretty deep -- she thinks -- answer to share 👀

That's what makes building so fun! All these small bits and pieces come together into a whole, that represent her as a person, regardless if her name is stamped on top of it :)

## Her origin story

Angela first started "coding" in grade 5 when her mom enrolled her in some online Chinese Scratch course. She really liked making games! Then in grade 10 & 11, she learned Java in high school CS class and made even cooler games. TBH she loved playing games just as much haha

In grade 11, she joined her high school's VEX robotics team and absolutely FELL IN LOVEEE with building robots. She has a distinct memory of staying up until 5am the night before a competition to work on the robot, only to oversleep her alarm the next morning :')

Her co-captain was actually much more of a coder than her. But naturally, she chose Mechatronics Engineering when university acceptances came around.

Side note here, she actually couldn't choose between Computer Engineering vs. Tron. The tipping point for her was because she liked Waterloo campus better LMAO

In her first 4 months of university, she was instantly **humbled** by the job search. Luckily, she ended up getting an offer to Ericsson towards the end of the term (shoutout)! She learned lots about Docker, Kubernetes, and so on... But the best part of the job was simply coding. She loved just being able to code.

Thankfully 95% of this job was AI-less so she actually coded, unlike now...

This is also when she decided to start her social media account! Becoming an influencer was always a childhood dream and living away from friends removed that fear of judgement of being cringe. So finally, for the first time, she seriously started on TikTok and Instagram.

The next 4 months of studying went by really quickly, with her landing a Shopify by a pure stroke of luck. She totally applied on a whim, but taking that offer might be one of the best decisions she's ever made.

She coded some more. She learned from INSANE 10x engineers on her team. She made friends who genuinely inspired her like never before. She challenged herself with crazy lore building activities. Everyday, she grew, she learned, and she was inspired. Read more here!

So she decided to transfer to Computer Engineering for a tad bit more specialization in SWE! Why UW CE? Um yeah because a friend really convinced her they enjoyed the program, and she was like yeah she'd go for it! Anyways, she's currently going through the thick of it with Computer Engineering T^T

## Her philosophy

- **Celebrate every small win:** Life is built on compounded results and rome wasn't built overnight.
- **Do it for the love of the game:** Results are cool but it's much cooler to love what you do ;)
- **Love life:** Only 1 life, so might as well love every bit of it. Some arcs are just a little harder than others.
- **"Is", not "was", not "will be":** Pretty flowers were never meant to last :)
- **Be unexpected and dangerous:** Life's not fun without a little surprise!
- **Enough:** Everything is enough. Life now is enough and is perfect.
- **Effort is the prize:** Participation for the win! Luck is always a factor, just keep showing up and learn.
