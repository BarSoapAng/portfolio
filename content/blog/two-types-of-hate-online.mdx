When I was posting content on Instagram, I got some pretty… interesting comments under my reels

People tell you “you know you really made it when you have haters” and I think this is true, but only half true.

Some creators, unfortunately, I believe genuinely deserve hate. Some people post slop, fraud, or just really REALLY bad takes. And in these cases, people will hate your content.

And sometimes, people will hate you as a person.

Now if you post genuinely bad content and get hate for it, I think this is a valid case to listen.

But if you are hated for “being cringe”, “being weird”, heck idk, for being a woman, tune that shit OUT. You know you the best and you have to be cringe, weird, different to eventually be cool, trendy, special.

Anyways, take with you what you make of this.

99% of people are consumers, it’s up to you to decide who to listen to
