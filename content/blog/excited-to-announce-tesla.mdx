I’ve turned my degree 180 to stand here.

To those who know me, you might know that I came to UW for mechatronics. You might also know I applied to many, MANY mech jobs before landing my first software role (shoutout Ericsson!)

And after one year of tron, I decided transferred to computer engineering, practically on a whim. This added 5 courses to my grad requirements.

Then, literally by pure miracle (and with insane not so miracle help from Julian Yam ), I got this offer.

And by another quick decision, I delayed my graduation by another year to be here (don’t get me started on the visa…)

But I can’t lie, I’m so goddamn excited. By the fact that I’ll go back to school and not know a single soul. By the way that this is my path forged from my decisions. And by the stories I’ll tell 20 years down the line.

So FUCK YEAH to saying “yes”
