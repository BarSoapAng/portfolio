Not because it’s “𝘣𝘢𝘥 𝘮𝘢𝘳𝘬𝘦𝘵𝘪𝘯𝘨” (although it is).
But because it’s killing what I love about creating.

As a small content creator and avid scroller, I look for **authenticity**. I love when people pour their souls into videos: telling stories, sharing value, building a community. I admire creators who put pieces of themselves in what they make.

**UGC is transactional.**
Creators don’t make UGC because they believe in the product -- they do it because it pays.

There’s creators with PAGES dedicated to making UGC for a single brand. Every video is maximized for virality, and that, to me, feels inauthentic.
I don’t want to spend hours on a video that lives on a brand’s page with no personality, no community, no 𝘮𝘦.

If you want to create — 𝘳𝘦𝘢𝘭𝘭𝘺 create — remember:
Your worth isn’t in conversion rates. It’s in connection.

Because at the end of the day,
**Storytelling is art.
UGC is advertisement.*
And there’s a difference.
