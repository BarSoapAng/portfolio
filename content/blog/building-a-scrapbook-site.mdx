I wanted the site to feel like opening a desk drawer full of saved notes, stickers, and receipts. A clean layout was not the goal. The goal was texture, motion, and personality.

## Why the rough edges matter

Perfectly smooth interfaces can be forgettable. I like pages that show decisions. The chunky borders, gradients, and little animated details make the site feel closer to a keepsake than a template.

## What I am protecting

I want future changes to keep three things intact:

- the sense that everything was placed intentionally
- a color palette that still feels bright and warm
- enough room for playful details without making the site hard to use

That balance is the interesting part of the work.
