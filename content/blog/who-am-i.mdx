# Hello World!

I first started "coding" in grade 5 when my mom enrolled me in some Chinese online Scratch course. I really liked making games! Then in grade 10 & 11, I learned Java in high school CS class and made even cooler games. 

In grade 11, I also joined my high school's VEX robotics team and absolutely FELL IN LOVEEE with building robots. I have a distinct memory of staying up until 5am the night before a competition to work on the robot, only to miss my alarm the next morning :')

My [co-captain](https://www.linkedin.com/in/oliver-huang-931947232/) was actually much more of a coder than me. So naturally, I chose Mechatronics Engineering when university acceptances came around.

In my first 4 months of university, I was instantly **humbled** by the job search. Luckily, I ended up getting an offer to Ericsson towards the end of the term (shoutout)! I learned lots about Docker, Kubernetes, you know... But the best part was simply coding. I loved just being able to code.

This is also when I decided to start my social media account! Becoming an influencer was always a childhood dream and living away from friends removed that fear of judgement of being cringe. So finally, for the first time, I seriously started on TikTok and Instagram. Read more here!

My next 4 months of studying went by really quickly, with me landing a Shopify by a pure stroke of luck. I totally applied on a whim, but taking that offer might be one of the best decisions I've ever made.

I coded some more. I learned from INSANE 10x engineers on my team. I made friends who genuinely inspire me like never before. I challenged myself with crazy lore building activities. Everyday, I grew, I learned, and I was inspired. Read more here!

So I decided to finally transfer for a tad bit more specialization in SWE! Anyways, I'm currently going through the thick of it with Computer Engineering T^T
