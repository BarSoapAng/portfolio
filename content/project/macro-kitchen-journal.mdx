Macro Kitchen Journal started as scattered notes and screenshots, then turned into one organized project with clear structure.

## Core idea

Every recipe needs three things:

- macro breakdown
- prep time reality check
- taste score after repeat attempts

## Implementation direction

- MDX entries for each recipe experiment
- short summary cards for browsing
- long-form detail pages for ingredient swaps and outcomes

## Result

Instead of re-learning the same lessons every week, I keep a reusable playbook and improve meals over time.
