This tracker was built for one purpose: fewer moving pieces, better visibility.

## Problem

Campaign details lived across chat messages, notes, and email threads.  
That caused missed handoffs and duplicate updates.

## Approach

- one central board for campaign status
- consistent naming for deliverables
- quick notes for blockers and approvals

## Outcome

The process became easier to trust because updates were visible and simple to maintain.
