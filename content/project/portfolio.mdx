## What I built

Angela's Universe is the portfolio you are exploring now. It is designed to feel collected and personal: warm paper, dusty pink, hand-drawn headings, botanical details, a vinyl player, and project Polaroids that can be dragged across a curved carousel.

The visual personality sits on top of a practical publishing system, so projects, work history, and writing can all be maintained as local MDX.

## Technical highlights

- Next.js App Router with server-rendered MDX collections
- local content images shared between indexes and detail pages
- styled-components design tokens and global theming
- Framer Motion interactions with reduced-motion support
- server-side Spotify playback integration
