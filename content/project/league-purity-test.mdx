## 100 questions, zero dignity

There is a purity test for basically everything, so obviously League of Legends needed one too.

I collected 100 increasingly questionable League experiences into one checklist. Every box you tick drags your purity score a little lower, and the final damage can be shared through its own results URL or posted straight to X.

The funniest part is that people actually showed up. More than 50,000 people took the test in its first two weeks! At peak, it handled 27,000+ requests in a day with zero downtime and average latency under 50ms. Not bad for a website dedicated to exposing League players LOL

## Under the hood

- a responsive React + Vite single-page app
- score calculation across all 100 tiny confessions
- shareable results saved in URL query parameters
- custom Google Analytics events for submissions and shares
- search metadata, a sitemap, and robots configuration so people could actually find it
- Cloudflare deployment that stayed fast through the traffic spike
